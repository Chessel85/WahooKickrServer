 
Reverse Engineering Dossier: Wahoo KICKR Gen 1 (v1.5.2)
Target Hardware: Wahoo KICKR Generation 1
Firmware Version: 1.5.2 (Confirmed via diagnostic string 05 02 02)
Communication Protocol: Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
Primary Service: a026ee01-0a7d-4ab3-97fa-f1500f9feb8b (Wahoo Specific)
Control Characteristic: a026e002-0a7d-4ab3-97fa-f1500f9feb8b (Read/Write/Notify)
 
1. Connection & Security (SUCCESS)
The device requires a specific handshake to unlock the Control Point. Standard FTMS or Wahoo "simple" writes return 05 (Access Denied) without this.
• 
Step 1 (Unlock): Write [0x20, 0xEE, 0xFC] to the Control Characteristic.
• 
Step 2 (Authorize): Write [0x03] to the same characteristic.
• 
Success Signature: Device notifies with 01 03 09. (Byte 09 is the confirmation of Master Control).
2. Live Data Reading (SUCCESS)
• 
Service: 0x1818 (Cycling Power Service).
• 
Characteristic: 0x2a63 (Cycling Power Measurement).
• 
Data Format: Uint16 Little Endian at offset 2 provides instantaneous Watts. This is verified and functional.
3. Resistance & Power Control (FAILED/PENDING)
This is the current "brick wall." Despite having "Master Control," the trainer is not physically engaging the electromagnetic brake.
Syntactic Results from Probes:
• 
Wahoo Standard (OpCodes 0x41/0x42): * Sending [0x41, level] returns 01 41 02 (Invalid Parameter).
• 
Sending [0x42, watts] returns 01 42 05 (Condition Not Met).
• 
FTMS/Standard Control (OpCodes 0x01-0x05):
• 
[0x04, value] (Set Resistance) returns 02 00 (Accepted/Processed).
• 
[0x05, value] (Set Power) returns 01 05 06 (Request Failed).
• 
[0x07] (Start/Resume) is acknowledged but does not seemingly change the outcome of subsequent 0x05 commands.
Current Hypotheses for Claude to Investigate:
1. 
Bit-Depth/Scaling: The 0x04 command returns success (02 00), but even high values (200+) result in no physical brake change. Is the scaling 14-bit? Is there a different byte-order required?
2. 
Mode Switching: Firmware 1.5.2 may require a specific "Set Mode" command (perhaps OpCode 0x46 or a sub-parameter of 0x42) to transition from "Passive" to "Active" before the brake energizes.
3. 
The "Silent" Success: Since [0x04, 0x32] returned 02 00, the trainer believes it did something. We might be writing to the wrong characteristic for the physical actuation, or it may require a simultaneous "Keep Alive" heartbeat.
 
Relevant Observations:
• 
The trainer responds to "Search Probes" (OpCodes 0x00 to 0x45).
• 
In one test, the pedaling got noticeably easier after a sweep, suggesting a Reset or Stop command (0x01 or 0x05) is functional.
• 
The trainer seems to be mimicking the Fitness Machine Service (FTMS) behavior but hosted on the Wahoo UUIDs.
